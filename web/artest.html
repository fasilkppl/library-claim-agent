AR depth test

Checks whether this phone's AR gives true distances. Results are logged on the Mac.

  1. Put a tape mark on the floor exactly 1 m from a plain wall.
  2. Tap Start. Move the phone slowly left and right for 5 seconds until it says tracking.
  3. Stand on the mark, point the red circle at the wall, hold still 5 seconds.
  4. Step back to 2 m, hold still 5 seconds. Then tap Stop.
