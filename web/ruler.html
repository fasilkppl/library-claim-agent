Screen ruler

Step 1, calibrate: lay any bank card (debit, credit or PVC Aadhaar: all exactly 8.56 cm wide) flat on the screen, its left edge on the left edge of the blue box. Move the slider until the box is exactly as wide as the card.

Step 2, measure: hold the book against the screen with its end on 0 and read the other end. Long ticks every cm, short ticks every mm.

Keep the browser zoom at 100% (Cmd+0). The calibration is remembered on this computer.
